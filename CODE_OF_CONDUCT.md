# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for contributors and users regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect confidential vulnerability reports and personal information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Give credit for research, code, and security findings.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate disruption, plagiarism, and publishing confidential security information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants. Maintainers must handle reports confidentially and avoid participating in decisions where they have a conflict of interest.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
